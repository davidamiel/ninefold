## Remaining gaps

- Emily M. Ferguson
- Helen Norton
- Jeremy Schepers
- Kevin Joel Page
- Vivek Suri

These gaps remain initials/neutral avatars. Searches covered employer and university biographies, professional directories, Oyez, conference materials and image search. Texas Bar and Oyez had no portraits for Page or Schepers; the available Ferguson student photo was rejected as unsuitable, as were unrelated namesakes. Every located Norton biography reused the same lecture candid, which was also rejected. No candid or generated substitute was installed.

The December and January argument calendars checked did not identify argument counsel. No future speaker was inferred from a brief signature alone.

## Identity and data corrections

Advocate appearances and roles were filled for ten argued OT26 cases from their Oyez case records (the existing three November records already identify counsel). Aimee Brown is normalized to Aimee W. Brown in speaker lookups; Mr. Hammach resolves to Joshua I. Hammack. Neil Gorsuch remains a justice alias, not an advocate.

Portrait source links are retained in the app credits. A publicly accessible photo is not assigned an invented open license. Existing licensed metadata is retained only for the unchanged Clement photo.
